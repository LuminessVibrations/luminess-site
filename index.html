Kim's Journey

Sound found me before I found it

Luminess Vibrations was born from a simple truth: we are, each of us, made of vibration. For over a decade, my voice was shaped by limiting beliefs and the fear of being seen, heard, and judged. I had always loved to sing, but somewhere along the way I began to silence the very thing that wanted to be expressed.

Through deep self-exploration, vocal work, vulnerability, and shared experiences, I began to find my way back to my voice — and discovered the liberating power of sound. Then came the moments of singing around fires, voices weaving together beneath the open sky. Something about those experiences lit me up from the inside. There was joy, freedom, and a feeling of belonging to something larger than myself.

Those moments sparked a desire to create spaces where people could experience that same aliveness — to sing, express, listen, and simply be together. Spaces where we can let go of the filtering, the performing, and the pressure to be a certain way, and feel safe enough to be fully ourselves.

As I continued exploring sound, I noticed how deeply it affected me — not only emotionally, but in my nervous system. Sound gave me a way to slow down, settle, express what words couldn't always reach, and feel more at home in my body. I realized sound was more than something I heard. It was something I could feel, embody, and experience — a language of vibration and a pathway back to myself.

And from that exploration, Luminess Vibrations emerged — a space to listen inward, explore your own resonance, and experience what becomes possible when you no longer have to filter your voice.

Guided by sound. Inspired by nature. Rooted in curiosity.
May we remember again & again.
